WELCOME TO THE CITY

A noir mob RPG. You start as a nobody. You end as a name people are afraid to say — or a body in the river. That part's up to you.

The loop: pull jobs for cash → train your muscle, cunning & speed → bank what you earn (pocket cash gets jumped) → join a family → set up earners that pay while you sleep → climb, scheme, and take the city.

Two things that matter: respect is your level — everything gates on it. And when you die, your street dies with you, but your account (prestige, $OMR, gear, your legit portfolio) survives to your heir. Play like it counts.

The Start Here tab is your first-week checklist — it pays cash to teach you the ropes. Lost? Tap the ? up top for the glossary. Now go make your bones.

THE LAY OF THE LAND

Respect / Level
Your standing. Respect is XP; your level is derived from it and gates every job, path, and perk.
Energy / Nerve
Energy fuels most actions (crimes, training). Nerve is spent on the riskier plays. Both refill over time.
Health / Hospital
Lose a fight and you're hospitalized for a spell. Hit zero in the wrong fight and you're dead.
Heat / The Law
Crime draws heat. Enough of it and the Bureau opens a RICO case — see The Law tab. Lay low or bribe to cool it.
Cash vs. the Bank vs. $OMR
Pocket cash can be looted when you're killed. Banked cash is safer (but takes time to clear). $OMR is the premium, launderable, extractable currency.
Omertà
The code of silence — your family won't hunt you, and you can't contract a made brother… unless they turn rat.
The Kitchen / Deal
Cook product, hire a crew, and move weight — the deepest cash engine. Watch the heat and the nut (crew wages).
Going Legit
Wash dirty cash into clean $OMR, then into a real blue-chip portfolio that survives your death. The endgame.
Prestige / The Heir
Death isn't the end of your account — your heir inherits prestige, $OMR, gear, and your legit book. Only the street resets.
